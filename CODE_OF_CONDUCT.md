# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want `codex-channels` to be an open, respectful, and constructive project.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- being respectful and patient
- giving constructive technical feedback
- assuming good intent while being direct about problems
- focusing discussion on the code, design, and user impact

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- harassment, insults, or discrimination
- bad-faith disruption
- publishing private information without permission
- hostile or abusive conduct in issues, reviews, or discussions

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and discussions that violate this code of conduct.
